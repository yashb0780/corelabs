## When to use

When a deal needs more than one thread. A migration is approved by a committee, and the seat you have not met is usually the one that says no.

## Instructions

1. Take the four seats in order: CIO, CFO, SAP CoE lead, IT procurement.
2. Fill each seat with the contact whose title fits it best. Use each contact once.
3. Mark the likely champion.
4. For every empty seat, say it is not identified yet.

**Constraints**

- A finance operations lead can sit in the CFO seat when no CFO is on record. Say that it is a stand-in.
- Never invent a name.

## Output format

One line per seat, then the contacts left over.
