## When to use

Monday morning, to decide where the week's outreach goes.

## Instructions

1. List every posting opened in the last seven days, newest first, with the account and team.
2. Then list the Mobilizing accounts, with their why-now line. These are the accounts where a partner seat is being decided.

**Constraints**

- Only postings seven days old or newer count as this week.

## Output format

Two sections: New postings this week, and Accounts to act on.
