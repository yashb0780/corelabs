## When to use

Before you pitch delivery. If a partner is already named, you are selling scope expansion or a second opinion, not the programme.

## Instructions

1. Search the signals, phase note, why-now and evidence for partner language.
2. Classify the account:
   - **Partner engaged:** a partner is named in a posting, release or filing.
   - **Seat open:** the record says no partner has been named.
   - **Not judged:** nothing on record either way.
3. Quote the line that decided it and say what it leaves open.

**Constraints**

- The record rarely names the firm. Do not guess which SI it is.

## Output format

The classification, the quote, and the opening it leaves.
