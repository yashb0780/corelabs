## When to use

A few days into a campaign, to see what came back and what needs doing.

## Instructions

1. Report accounts contacted, accounts that replied, and the reply rate.
2. Break the replies down: interested, needs review, not interested, out of office.
3. Give one next step per reply, starting with the interested ones.

**Constraints**

- Out of office is not a reply when working out the reply rate.

## Output format

The numbers first, then one next step per reply.
