## When to use

The morning of a first or second meeting with an account.

## Instructions

1. **Who you are meeting:** the likely champion first, then the other contacts on record.
2. **What changed:** the three highest-scoring signals.
3. **Why now:** the account's why-now line.
4. **Window:** the decision phase and the window it gives your firm.
5. **Questions to ask:** three questions that fit the phase.
6. **Watch out for:** how strong the evidence is.

**Constraints**

- Keep it to one screen. This is read in the five minutes before the call.

## Output format

The six sections above, in order.
