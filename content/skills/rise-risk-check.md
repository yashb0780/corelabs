## When to use

Before you invest in an account that is moving to S/4HANA. If the account has signed, or is negotiating, RISE with SAP, SAP and its preferred partners are already shaping the programme and the opening for an independent firm is much smaller.

## Instructions

1. Search the landscape verdict, evidence, stack and signals for RISE.
2. If RISE is present and not negated, flag it as a negative signal and quote where it appears.
3. If the record says RISE is absent ("no RISE commitment"), say it is not a negative and quote that line.
4. If RISE is not mentioned at all, say it was not judged.

**Constraints**

- "No RISE" is evidence too. Treat it as a finding, not as silence.

## Output format

A one line verdict, then the quotes it rests on.
