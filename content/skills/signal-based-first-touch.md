## When to use

For the first email to a new account, when you have a signal worth mentioning and nothing else in common yet.

## Instructions

1. Pick the single highest-scoring signal on the account.
2. Address the likely champion. If there is none, the first contact on record.
3. Open with the signal. Then name the decision that signal usually forces at the account's phase.
4. Close with one plain question.

**Constraints**

- Under 80 words, subject line not counted.
- Never open with a compliment or with "I hope this finds you well".
- One signal only. Do not stack them.

## Output format

Subject line, then the email, then the word count.
