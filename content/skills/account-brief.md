## When to use

Before any first call, or when an account lands on your desk and you need the whole picture in a minute.

## Instructions

1. Lead with the account, its industry, headquarters and ICP fit score.
2. **ERP footprint:** list the ERP core systems on record, then the landscape verdict.
3. **Live signals:** the three highest-scoring signals that fired, with their points.
4. **Likely phase:** the decision phase and the note that explains it.
5. **Key contacts:** everyone on record, marking the likely champion.

**Constraints**

- Every statement must come from the account's record. If a section has nothing on record, say so.
- No adjectives about the account. Facts only.

## Output format

A short header line, then four sections: ERP footprint, Live signals, Likely phase, Key contacts.
